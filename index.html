About

A philosopher of futures in an age of intelligent systems.

Dr. Hamid’s career connects philosophy, education, technology, foresight and public dialogue. His work helped bring Futures Studies, Critical Thinking and Ethics into university learning while his recent scholarship explores how human judgment, uncertainty and responsibility should evolve alongside AI.

Teaching & Mentorship Legacy

Educating generations, not only delivering courses.

His teaching career reaches back to the University of Karachi in 1990. Across more than three decades, Dr. Hamid has taught, supervised, mentored and designed learning environments for students, researchers, faculty and education leaders.

Three decades of university teaching

From philosophy at the University of Karachi and Hamdard University to postgraduate supervision, curriculum leadership and deanship at Iqra University, teaching has remained a continuous thread in his career.

10,000+ learners through IMAGINE

Public biographical records report that IMAGINE – Institute of Futures Studies has trained more than 10,000 learners since 2019 under his founding leadership, extending Futures Literacy beyond a single classroom.

Mentorship across generations

His influence extends through students, emerging futurists, researchers, teachers and institutional leaders. The 10,000+ figure is presented as institutional reach under his leadership, not as an unverifiable claim that he personally taught every learner.
