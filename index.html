Academic profile

Inquiry before certainty.

Dr. Hamid’s intellectual work is grounded in philosophy and shaped by decades of teaching, education reform and institutional leadership. His current agenda brings that foundation into conversation with Futures Literacy, strategic foresight and the governance of increasingly capable AI systems.

Rather than treating intelligence as answer production, his recent writing asks how judgment, refusal, traceability, reciprocity and human agency should operate when knowledge is uncertain and decisions carry public consequences.
